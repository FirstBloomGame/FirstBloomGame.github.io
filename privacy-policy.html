First Bloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The short version

  • First Bloom has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device. We never receive it.
  • The game shows optional ads from Google AdMob, but only if you tap "Watch an ad". Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as explained below.
  • If you're in the EU, UK or Switzerland, you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings.

Who we are

First Bloom is an idle evolution game made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy or your privacy, email us at firstbloomgame@outlook.com.

What the game stores on your device

To remember your progress, First Bloom saves a small file on your device. It contains your game progress, your sound and music settings, and simple statistics about how you play, such as how many times you've tapped. This file never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

First Bloom offers optional "rewarded" ads. For example, after you've been away you can choose to watch an ad to double your earnings. Ads are never shown unless you tap a button asking for one.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When the game starts, and when an ad is loaded or shown, Google's ad software in the game may collect and process the following data. Google uses it to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud:

DataWhat it isWhy
Device identifiersYour device's advertising ID and app set IDAdvertising, measuring ads, fraud prevention
Approximate locationWorked out from your IP address (not GPS)Showing ads that fit your region, fraud prevention
Ad interactionsWhether an ad was loaded, shown, watched or tappedMeasuring ads, fraud prevention
DiagnosticsTechnical data such as crash logs and performanceKeeping ads working properly

This data is sent securely (encrypted) to Google. We do not receive it, and we can't see who you are. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Ads in First Bloom are limited to content suitable for teens.

Your choices

  • Don't watch ads. Ads are entirely optional, and the game is fully playable without them.
  • Personalized ads (EU, UK and Switzerland). Before any ads load, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to personalized ads and related data use. You can change your answer later in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • US state privacy laws. Where required, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for ads through the same Settings → Ad privacy choices option.
  • Android settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or limit its use, and to withdraw consent. Because First Bloom doesn't collect or store personal data on our side, we hold no personal data about you to hand over or delete. Your game save stays on your device, and you can delete it yourself as described above. For ad data processed by Google, see Google's privacy policy linked above. You can also contact us with any request, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal basis (EU, UK and Switzerland)

Ad-related data is processed by Google based on your consent, which you give or refuse through the consent form described above. Where you refuse consent for personalization, Google may still show limited, non-personalized ads as permitted by law. Saving your game on your device is necessary to provide the game you asked to play.

Children

First Bloom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we'll help.

Data retention

Your save file stays on your device until you reset your progress or uninstall the game. Google keeps ad data according to its own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above. If a change is significant, for example if the game starts collecting new kinds of data, we'll also mention it in the game's update notes.

Contact
